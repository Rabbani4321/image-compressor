GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What does compression do?

Compression changes image data storage to reduce size. Lossy formats discard some information; PNG is lossless and may not become smaller.

What does quality change?

Quality controls size versus detail for WebP and JPEG. Browsers ignore it for PNG.

Which formats and transparency behavior should I expect?

Input accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP. WebP supports transparency, but lossy encoding can change fine detail and transparent edges. JPEG has no transparency and composites it onto white. PNG is lossless and keeps transparency.

Are images uploaded or stored?

No. Images are read and encoded locally by your browser and are not sent to a remote service.

Why can output be larger?

Some images are already optimized or do not compress well in the chosen format. Larger output is reported without claiming savings.
